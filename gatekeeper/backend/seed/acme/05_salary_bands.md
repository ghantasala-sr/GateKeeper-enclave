# Acme Corp Engineering Salary Bands (2026)

**Confidential. HR managers and admins only.**

All figures are annual base salary in USD for US-based employees.

| Level | Title | Minimum | Midpoint | Maximum |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| E1 | Software Engineer I | $95,000 | $105,000 | $115,000 |
| E2 | Software Engineer II | $115,000 | $127,500 | $140,000 |
| E3 | Senior Software Engineer | $135,000 | $150,000 | $165,000 |
| E4 | Staff Software Engineer | $170,000 | $187,500 | $205,000 |
| E5 | Principal Software Engineer | $210,000 | $230,000 | $250,000 |

## Senior Engineer Band
The **Acme senior software engineer band midpoint is $150,000**, with a range of $135,000 to $165,000.

## Location Adjustment
Austin-based roles receive a 5% uplift on all band figures.

## Bonus Targets
- E1–E2: 5% of base
- E3: 8% of base
- E4–E5: 12% of base

## Rules
Offers above the band maximum require approval from the VP of People and the CFO.
