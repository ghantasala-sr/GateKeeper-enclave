# Acme Corp Performance Review Guidelines

**Confidential. HR managers and admins only.**

## Review Cycle
Acme runs reviews twice a year: in **March** and **September**.

## Rating Scale
| Rating | Meaning | Target share of employees |
|---|---|---|
| 5 | Exceptional | 10% |
| 4 | Exceeds expectations | 25% |
| 3 | Meets expectations | 55% |
| 2 | Partially meets | 8% |
| 1 | Does not meet | 2% |

## Calibration
Managers bring proposed ratings to a calibration meeting led by the HR business partner. Ratings of 5 or 1 need written examples.

## Pay Outcomes
- Rating 5: 8–10% merit increase
- Rating 4: 5–7% merit increase
- Rating 3: 3% merit increase
- Rating 2 or 1: no merit increase, and a performance improvement plan is considered

## Performance Improvement Plans
A PIP lasts **60 days** at Acme. HR must review every PIP before it is shared with the employee.
