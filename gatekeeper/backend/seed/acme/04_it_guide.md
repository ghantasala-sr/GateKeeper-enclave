# Acme Corp IT Guide

## Logging In
All Acme apps use single sign-on through **Okta**. Use your Acme email and password, then approve the Okta Verify push on your phone.

## Your Laptop
New hires receive a 14-inch MacBook Pro. Laptops are refreshed every 3 years.

## Passwords
- At least 14 characters
- Changed only if you suspect it was exposed
- Never shared, even with IT

## Getting Help
- Slack: #acme-it-help
- Email: helpdesk@acme.example
- Phone: extension 4357 (HELP)

The Help Desk is staffed 7:00 AM to 7:00 PM Eastern, Monday through Friday.

## Reporting a Security Problem
If you click a suspicious link or lose your laptop, report it to security@acme.example **within 1 hour**. You will not be punished for reporting quickly.
