# Acme Corp Paid Time Off Policy

## How Much PTO You Get
Full-time Acme employees receive **20 days of PTO per year**, accrued each pay period. After 5 years of service, this increases to 25 days.

## Company Holidays
Acme observes 10 paid company holidays, including a company-wide shutdown between December 24 and January 1.

## Carryover
You may carry over up to **5 unused days** into the next calendar year. Anything above 5 days is forfeited on January 1.

## Requesting Time Off
Submit requests in Workday at least 2 weeks ahead for absences of 3 days or more. Shorter absences need 2 business days of notice.

## Sick Leave
Sick leave is separate from PTO. Employees get 8 sick days per year, and a doctor's note is required only after 3 consecutive sick days.

## Leaving Acme
Accrued, unused PTO is paid out in your final paycheck.
