# Acme Corp Q3 2026 Financial Plan

**Executive confidential. Admins only.**

## Revenue Target
Q3 2026 revenue target: **$42.5 million**, up 12% from Q3 2025.

| Segment | Q3 target |
|---|---|
| Sensor hardware | $27.0M |
| Software subscriptions | $11.5M |
| Services | $4.0M |

## Margins
- Gross margin target: 48%
- Operating margin target: 9%

## Operating Expenses
Total Q3 operating expenses are budgeted at **$16.6 million**, with $7.2M going to R&D.

## Cash
Expected cash balance at end of Q3: **$31 million**.

## Key Risks
- Chip supply delays could push $3M of hardware revenue into Q4.
- The largest customer, Midwest Rail, is renegotiating its contract.
