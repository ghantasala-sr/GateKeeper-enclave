# Acquisition Memo: Project Falcon

**Executive confidential. Admins only. Do not forward.**

## Target
Acme plans to acquire **Pinecrest Robotics**, a 60-person company that builds robotic inspection arms.

## Proposed Terms
- Purchase price: **$18 million**
- 70% cash, 30% Acme stock
- $2 million retention pool for Pinecrest engineers over 2 years

## Why
Pinecrest's arms pair with Acme sensors to offer a complete automated inspection product. We expect $6M in new annual revenue by 2028.

## Timeline
- Letter of intent: September 2026
- Due diligence: October to November 2026
- Target close: **December 15, 2026**

## Risks
Pinecrest's two founders hold key patents. Their retention agreements are a condition of closing.
