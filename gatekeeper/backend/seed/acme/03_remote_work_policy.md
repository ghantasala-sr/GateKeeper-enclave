# Acme Corp Remote Work Policy

## Hybrid Schedule
Acme is a hybrid company. Employees assigned to Dayton or Austin work **in the office 3 days a week**: Tuesday, Wednesday, and Thursday. Monday and Friday are flexible.

## Fully Remote Roles
Some roles are approved as fully remote. These must be approved by a VP and listed in the job offer.

## Home Office Stipend
Hybrid and remote employees receive a one-time **$500 home office stipend** for a desk, chair, or monitor.

## Security When Working Remotely
- Use only your Acme-issued laptop for work.
- Connect through the Acme VPN when accessing internal systems.
- Do not work from public Wi-Fi without the VPN.

## Working From Another Country
Working outside the United States for more than 14 days requires approval from People Ops and Legal because of tax and data rules.
