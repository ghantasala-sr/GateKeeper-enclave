# Acme Corp Board Meeting Notes (August 2026)

**Executive confidential. Admins only.**

## Attendees
CEO Dana Whitfield, CFO Marcus Lee, and five board directors.

## Decisions
1. Approved the Q3 financial plan ($42.5M revenue target).
2. Approved negotiations to acquire Pinecrest Robotics (see the acquisition memo).
3. Approved a **$5 million** share buyback program over 12 months.

## Discussion
- The board asked for a plan to reduce dependence on Midwest Rail, which is 22% of revenue.
- Directors raised concern about rising chip costs and asked for a supplier diversification update in November.

## Executive Changes
The board approved the search for a Chief Technology Officer. Target start date: January 2027.
