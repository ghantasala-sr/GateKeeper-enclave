# Acme Corp Employee Handbook

## Welcome
Welcome to Acme Corp. We build industrial sensors and the software that runs them. Acme has about 1,200 employees across our Dayton, Ohio headquarters and our Austin, Texas engineering office.

## Our Values
- **Build it right.** Quality beats speed when safety is involved.
- **Say what you see.** Raise problems early, even if they are uncomfortable.
- **Own the outcome.** If you start it, see it through.

## Working Hours
Core hours are 10:00 AM to 3:00 PM Eastern. Outside core hours, schedule your day with your manager.

## Code of Conduct
Treat coworkers, customers, and partners with respect. Harassment and discrimination are not tolerated. Report concerns to your manager, to People Ops at people@acme.example, or through the anonymous Acme Ethics Line.

## Benefits Overview
- Medical, dental, and vision coverage starting on day one
- 401(k) with a 4% company match
- $1,000 per year learning budget
- Paid parental leave of 14 weeks

## Getting Help
People Ops answers handbook questions. The IT Help Desk handles laptops, accounts, and access.
