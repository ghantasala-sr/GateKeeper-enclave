# Acme Corp 2026 Hiring Plan

**Confidential. HR managers and admins only.**

## Headcount Summary
Acme plans to grow from 1,200 to **1,340 employees** by the end of 2026, a net increase of 140.

| Department | Current | Planned hires | End of year |
|---|---|---|---|
| Engineering | 410 | 75 | 485 |
| Manufacturing | 380 | 30 | 410 |
| Sales | 160 | 20 | 180 |
| Customer Support | 120 | 10 | 130 |
| G&A | 130 | 5 | 135 |

## Priority Roles
1. Embedded firmware engineers (20 openings)
2. Senior software engineers for the cloud platform (15 openings)
3. Field sales engineers in the Midwest region (8 openings)

## Recruiting Budget
Total 2026 recruiting budget is **$2.1 million**, including agency fees capped at 18% of first-year salary.

## Hiring Freeze Trigger
If Q3 revenue lands more than 10% below plan, non-engineering hiring pauses automatically.
