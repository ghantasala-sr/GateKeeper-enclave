# Globex 2026 Hiring Plan

**Confidential. HR managers and admins only.**

## Headcount Summary
Globex plans to grow from 2,300 to **2,610 employees** by the end of 2026, a net increase of 310.

| Department | Current | Planned hires | End of year |
|---|---|---|---|
| Engineering | 940 | 180 | 1,120 |
| Product & Design | 210 | 35 | 245 |
| Sales | 480 | 60 | 540 |
| Customer Success | 390 | 25 | 415 |
| G&A | 280 | 10 | 290 |

## Priority Roles
1. Machine learning engineers for route optimization (30 openings)
2. Senior backend engineers (40 openings)
3. Enterprise account executives in Europe (12 openings)

## Recruiting Budget
Total 2026 recruiting budget is **$4.8 million**, with a referral bonus of $7,500 per engineering hire.

## Hiring Freeze Trigger
If net revenue retention falls below 105% for two quarters, all hiring outside engineering pauses.
