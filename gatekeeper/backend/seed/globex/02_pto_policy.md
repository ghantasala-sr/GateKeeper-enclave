# Globex Paid Time Off Policy

## How Much PTO You Get
Full-time Globex employees receive **25 days of PTO per year**, granted in full on January 1. After 4 years of service, this increases to 30 days.

## Company Holidays
Globex observes 11 paid company holidays, plus one floating holiday of your choice.

## Carryover
You may carry over up to **10 unused days** into the next calendar year. Carried-over days must be used by March 31.

## Requesting Time Off
Submit requests in BambooHR at least 1 week ahead for absences of 3 days or more. Shorter absences need 1 business day of notice.

## Sick Leave
Globex offers unlimited sick leave for short illnesses. Absences longer than 5 days move to short-term disability.

## Wellness Fridays
The last Friday of every month is a company-wide day off.
