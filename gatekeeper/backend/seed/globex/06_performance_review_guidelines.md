# Globex Performance Review Guidelines

**Confidential. HR managers and admins only.**

## Review Cycle
Globex runs one annual review in **November**, with a lighter check-in in May.

## Rating Scale
| Rating | Meaning | Target share of employees |
|---|---|---|
| Outstanding | Far above the role | 15% |
| Strong | Solidly above the role | 30% |
| Solid | Fully meets the role | 45% |
| Developing | Below the role | 10% |

## Calibration
Ratings are calibrated by department heads with the People Partner. Each "Outstanding" rating needs peer feedback from at least 3 coworkers.

## Pay Outcomes
- Outstanding: 10–12% merit increase plus a refresh equity grant
- Strong: 6–8% merit increase
- Solid: 4% merit increase
- Developing: no merit increase and a development plan

## Development Plans
Development plans at Globex last **90 days** and are coaching-focused. The People Partner joins the first and last check-in.
