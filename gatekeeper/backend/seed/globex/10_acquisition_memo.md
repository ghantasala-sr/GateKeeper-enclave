# Acquisition Memo: Project Lighthouse

**Executive confidential. Admins only. Do not forward.**

## Target
Globex plans to acquire **Halcyon Data**, a 140-person company that predicts port congestion using satellite and weather data.

## Proposed Terms
- Purchase price: **$95 million**
- 50% cash, 50% Globex stock
- $8 million retention pool for Halcyon's data science team over 3 years

## Why
Halcyon's congestion forecasts would make Globex route plans more accurate and justify a premium pricing tier. We expect $15M in new annual revenue by 2028.

## Timeline
- Letter of intent: August 2026
- Due diligence: September to November 2026
- Target close: **January 31, 2027**

## Risks
Halcyon depends on a single satellite imagery provider. Securing a long-term data contract is a condition of closing.
