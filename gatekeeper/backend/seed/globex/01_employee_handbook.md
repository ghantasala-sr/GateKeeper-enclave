# Globex Employee Handbook

## Welcome
Welcome to Globex. We make logistics software that helps shipping companies plan routes and track cargo. Globex has about 2,300 employees, with headquarters in Portland, Oregon and offices in Toronto and Dublin.

## Our Values
- **Customers first.** Every decision starts with the customer's problem.
- **Move fast, then measure.** Ship small, learn, and adjust.
- **Win together.** Credit is shared and blame is not.

## Working Hours
Globex has no fixed core hours. Teams agree on at least 3 hours of shared overlap each day.

## Code of Conduct
Respect everyone you work with. Discrimination, harassment, and retaliation are not tolerated. Report concerns to your manager, to the People Team at people@globex.example, or through the Globex SpeakUp line.

## Benefits Overview
- Medical, dental, and vision coverage starting on day one
- 401(k) with a 6% company match
- $2,500 per year learning budget
- Paid parental leave of 20 weeks

## Getting Help
The People Team answers handbook questions. Tech Support handles laptops, accounts, and access.
