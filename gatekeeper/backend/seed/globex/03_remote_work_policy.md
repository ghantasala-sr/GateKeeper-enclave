# Globex Remote Work Policy

## Remote First
Globex is a **remote-first** company. Employees may work from home full time. Offices in Portland, Toronto, and Dublin are open for anyone who prefers them.

## In-Person Gatherings
Each team meets in person once per quarter. Globex pays travel and lodging.

## Home Office Stipend
Remote employees receive a **$1,200 home office stipend** when they join, plus $75 per month for internet.

## Security When Working Remotely
- Use only your Globex-issued laptop for work.
- Keep your laptop's disk encryption turned on.
- Lock your screen whenever you step away.

## Working From Another Country
Employees may work from another country for up to 30 days per year. Longer stays need approval from the People Team.
