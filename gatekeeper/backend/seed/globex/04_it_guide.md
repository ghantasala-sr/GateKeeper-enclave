# Globex IT Guide

## Logging In
All Globex apps use single sign-on through **Microsoft Entra ID**. Sign in with your Globex email, then approve the Microsoft Authenticator prompt.

## Your Laptop
New hires choose between a Dell XPS 15 and a 16-inch MacBook Pro. Laptops are refreshed every 4 years.

## Passwords
- At least 16 characters
- Use the company password manager, 1Password
- Never shared, even with Tech Support

## Getting Help
- Teams channel: Globex Tech Support
- Email: techsupport@globex.example
- Phone: extension 8324 (TECH)

Tech Support is available 24 hours a day because Globex has staff in several time zones.

## Reporting a Security Problem
If you suspect phishing or lose a device, report it to security@globex.example **right away**. Fast reporting is always rewarded, never punished.
