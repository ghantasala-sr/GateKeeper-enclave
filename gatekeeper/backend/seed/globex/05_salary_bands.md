# Globex Engineering Salary Bands (2026)

**Confidential. HR managers and admins only.**

All figures are annual base salary in USD for US-based employees.

| Level | Title | Minimum | Midpoint | Maximum |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| L3 | Software Engineer | $110,000 | $122,500 | $135,000 |
| L4 | Software Engineer II | $135,000 | $147,500 | $160,000 |
| L5 | Senior Software Engineer | $155,000 | $170,000 | $185,000 |
| L6 | Staff Software Engineer | $190,000 | $210,000 | $230,000 |
| L7 | Principal Software Engineer | $235,000 | $260,000 | $285,000 |

## Senior Engineer Band
The **Globex senior software engineer band midpoint is $170,000**, with a range of $155,000 to $185,000.

## Location Adjustment
Globex pays the same band in every US location. Toronto and Dublin use separate local-currency bands.

## Equity
- L3–L4: $20,000 in RSUs per year
- L5: $40,000 in RSUs per year
- L6–L7: $80,000 in RSUs per year

## Rules
Offers above the band maximum require approval from the Chief People Officer.
